our story

A Detroit Kitchen Built on Faith, Flour & Love

From Scratch Kitchen started the way most good things do — at a family table in Detroit. A single mom cooking for her own, the way her family always had: from scratch, with patience, and with prayer.

Somewhere along the way, the world traded real cooking for shortcuts. Boxes. Freezers. Drive-thrus. And it shows — food stopped feeling like somebody cared about you.

This kitchen is the answer to that. Every pan that leaves here is made by hand, in the name of Jesus — wholesome food meant to feed more than your stomach.

"Taste and see that the Lord is good." — Psalm 34:8
