our story

A Detroit Kitchen Built on Faith, Flour & Love

From Scratch Kitchen started the way most good things do — at a family table in Detroit. A single mom who learned that food made with intention tastes completely different from food just thrown together.

The world got busy. Shortcuts got popular. And somewhere along the way, cooking from scratch started feeling like too much — when really, it's the most important thing we can do for the people we love.

This kitchen exists to bring that back. Classes that teach you the real way. Recipes that actually work. Food cooked for your celebrations like it matters — because it does.

"Taste and see that the Lord is good." — Psalm 34:8
— Rakia, From Scratch Kitchen
